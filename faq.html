Your guide to exploring BharatYatra

About BharatYatra

What is BharatYatra?

BharatYatra is a comprehensive India tourism platform that helps travelers discover destinations, plan itineraries, explore cultural experiences, and learn about medical tourism across India. Whether you're a first-time visitor or a seasoned explorer, we make your journey through India seamless and unforgettable.

Is BharatYatra free to use?

Yes, BharatYatra is completely free for all users. You can explore destinations, browse itineraries, and discover experiences without any charges. Our goal is to make India's beauty accessible to every traveler around the world.

Who is BharatYatra designed for?

BharatYatra is designed for everyone — solo backpackers, families, honeymooners, adventure seekers, spiritual travelers, and international tourists. We cater to all travel styles and budgets with curated content for each type of explorer.

Destinations & Experiences

What types of destinations can I explore on BharatYatra?

India is incredibly diverse — on BharatYatra you can explore tropical beaches in Goa and the Andamans, the Himalayan peaks of Ladakh and Himachal Pradesh, ancient heritage cities like Varanasi and Jaipur, dense wildlife sanctuaries like Ranthambore and Jim Corbett, and serene spiritual retreats in Rishikesh and Tirupati.

What is the "Colors of India" section?

Colors of India showcases the vibrant cultural tapestry of the country — from the golden deserts of Rajasthan to the lush green backwaters of Kerala, from the blue city of Jodhpur to the white salt flats of the Rann of Kutch. It's a visual and cultural journey through India's most striking and unique landscapes and traditions.

What kind of experiences does BharatYatra offer?

Our Experiences section covers a wide range — adventure sports like trekking, river rafting, and paragliding; cultural experiences like cooking classes, folk art workshops, and temple tours; wildlife safaris; yoga and wellness retreats; and immersive rural homestays that let you live like a local.

Which is the best time to visit India?

The best time depends on the region. October to March is ideal for most of India — the weather is pleasant and festivals like Diwali, Holi, and Pushkar Mela light up the country. Summer (April–June) is perfect for hill stations like Shimla and Manali. The monsoon season (July–September) is stunning for Kerala and the Western Ghats.

Planning Your Trip

How do I use the Itinerary section?

Our Itinerary section offers ready-made travel plans for popular routes — like the Golden Triangle (Delhi–Agra–Jaipur), the Kerala Backwaters circuit, or the Himalayan adventure trail. Each itinerary includes day-by-day breakdowns, must-visit stops, local tips, and estimated travel times to help you plan with confidence.

Do I need a visa to travel to India?

Most international travelers need a visa to visit India. India offers an e-Visa (electronic visa) for citizens of over 160 countries, which can be applied for online before your trip. We recommend applying at least 4–7 days before your travel date. Always check the latest requirements from the official Indian government visa portal for your specific nationality.

Is India safe for solo travelers and tourists?

India is generally safe for tourists when you take standard precautions. Popular destinations like Rajasthan, Kerala, Goa, and major cities are well-equipped for travelers. We recommend staying aware of your surroundings, using registered transport, keeping copies of your documents, and checking local advisories before visiting remote areas.

Travel for Life & Sustainability

What is "Travel for Life"?

Travel for Life is our sustainability initiative that promotes responsible tourism across India. It encourages travelers to respect local ecosystems, support local artisans and businesses, reduce plastic usage, and choose eco-friendly accommodations. We believe travel should leave destinations better than we found them.

How can I travel responsibly in India?

Traveling responsibly in India means respecting local customs and dress codes at religious sites, avoiding single-use plastics, buying from local vendors instead of large chains, not disturbing wildlife in sanctuaries, and learning a few words of the local language. Small actions make a big difference in preserving India's incredible heritage and nature.

Medical Tourism

What is medical tourism in India?

India is one of the world's top medical tourism destinations, offering world-class healthcare at a fraction of the cost compared to Western countries. Popular treatments include cardiac surgery, orthopedic procedures, dental care, fertility treatments, and Ayurvedic wellness therapies. Our Medical Tour section helps you discover reputed hospitals and wellness centers across India.

Which cities in India are known for medical tourism?

Chennai, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, and Bengaluru are India's top medical tourism hubs, home to JCI-accredited hospitals and internationally trained specialists. Kerala is world-renowned for Ayurveda and holistic wellness retreats, making it a unique destination for those seeking natural healing alongside their travel experience.
