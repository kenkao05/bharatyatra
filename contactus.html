About the Team

Meet the Makers

BharatYatra is a student project built as part of Design Engineering II-B at SAL College of Engineering, Ahmedabad. Our team of five Computer Engineering students set out to create a comprehensive travel discovery platform for India — covering destinations, itineraries, medical access, and cultural events across five states.
